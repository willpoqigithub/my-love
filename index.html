Always.

For You, Precious

Today is the first day of November,
and my heart is still the same —
it still chooses you.

I hope this month is gentle with you.
Please take care of yourself for me.
Rest when you need to.
Eat well. Take your medicine.
And never forget how deeply you are loved.

I’m here for the rest of this year,
and for all the years that will come after.

No matter how heavy the days get,
you never have to face them alone.

I love you.
More than these words can hold.
